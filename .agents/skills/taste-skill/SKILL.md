# Taste Skill — Anti-Slop Frontend Framework

## Core Directives
1. **No Vibe-Coded Slop**: Ban generic rainbow blur meshes, floating neon glow spheres, and excessive multi-gradient cards.
2. **Solid & Architectural**: Use solid, intentional background colors (deep obsidian, rich charcoal, crisp linen/warm white, crisp architectural hairline borders).
3. **Restrained Color Palette**: Max 2 core brand colors plus neutral scales. High contrast, accessible ratios (4.5:1 minimum).
4. **Editorial Typography Hierarchy**: Strong serif or modern geometric sans with tight tracking, balanced leading, and zero orphan line awkwardness.
5. **Physical & Tactile Motion**: Snappy cubic-bezier easings (Emil Kowalski style), active press feedback on all interactives, fast exit durations.
6. **Information Density & Craftsmanship**: Clean data layouts, structured deliverables, crisp tabular badges, and meaningful whitespace.
